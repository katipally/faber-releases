## Pairing your iPhone

1. On your Mac, open Faber, then Settings, Companion, Pair new iPhone.
2. On your iPhone, open Faber Companion and tap Pair with code. Scan the QR code or type the code.
3. No Mac handy? Tap Try the demo to explore the app with sample data.

## Alerts not arriving

- iPhone: Settings, Notifications, Faber Companion must be on.
- Mac: in Faber, Settings, Notifications, choose which events alert your iPhone.
- Alerts are sent by Faber for Mac, so it must be running on your Mac.
